1 · The institution
Mynta AB is a Swedish e-money institution, authorised in 2018, supervised by Finansinspektionen,
headquartered in Stockholm, 118 employees. It serves 152,400 consumers and 7,340 business
customers, of which 620 are acquiring merchants. 2025 processed volume: SEK 38.2bn. Revenue
SEK 412m; operating result SEK 31m.

Mynta operates no cash services, no branches, no agents, no correspondent relationships and no
trade finance. Onboarding is fully remote. Occasional transactions for non-customers are not
offered.


3 · Customers and geography
Customer base: 68% Sweden, 29% rest of EEA, 3% non-EEA. Merchant ownership chains reach
approximately 40 jurisdictions; 18,900 consumers and 41 business customers are crypto-active.
Monthly activity: 3,900 consumer onboardings, 140 business, 18 acquiring; 2,150 monitoring alerts;
5,400 screening name hits; 610 periodic reviews.


4 · Organisation and control
Governance follows GOV-02 section 5: board, designated executive, and the central function holder
with a compliance function of eleven. Three lines of defence per GOV-02 section 5.4; independent
examination per GOV-06. The operational model is procedure-driven: the SOP series defines
execution, REG-C holds operating values, and Kompass is the system of record for casework.


5 · Material third parties and outsourcing
CardPartner Europe Ltd (BIN sponsor), Norrbank AB (safeguarding of customer funds, average SEK
1,870m), a screening vendor and an identity vendor, governed under GOV-08. Group services with
Mynta Digital AB are documented between the entities per GOV-02 section 12. Responsibility for
regulatory compliance is never outsourced.


6 · Group boundary
Mynta Digital AB holds its own CASP authorisation and maintains its own regulatory documentation,
including custody, transfer-service and market-conduct frameworks, under MiCA and its technical
standards. That framework is internal to the group and outside this published pack. The interface
Mynta AB operates (distribution, fiat product area, travel-rule data flows, coordination) is governed by
the documents in this pack, principally GOV-02 section 12, SOP-17 and REG-C C-8.


7 · Financial and prudential arrangements
Own funds, capital monitoring, safeguarding arrangements and financial control operate under the
internal documents listed in REG-D (prudential and capital monitoring policy, accounting and
financial control policy, GOV-05 safeguarding). Safeguarded customer funds are held at Norrbank
AB under the segregation method with daily reconciliation.

8 · Forward plan
The operating plan is continuity: the four service lines at current scale, AMLR re-baselining through
SOP-25 ahead of July 2027, and no new products or markets without GOV-09 assessment. Growth
assumptions in planning are deliberately modest and carry no dependency in this framework.


What the FI application contains
A licence application to Finansinspektionen is submitted in Swedish. Where the working documents
are in English, the full pack is translated before filing; practice has tightened on this. The business
plan for a new institution typically lands at 30 to 40 pages, describing each requirement briefly and
referring to the full document for the detail. The table maps the application contents to where each
part lives.
