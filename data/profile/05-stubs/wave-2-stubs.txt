Full version in wave 2. The points below bind now; other documents cite them.



Purpose
Underwriting standards for acquiring merchants: business verification beyond KYB, MCC assignment
and C-7 enforcement, processing-history assessment, chargeback and refund expectations, and
settlement configuration.


Binding points
  • Every merchant passes SOP-10 in full before underwriting concludes. Underwriting adds to KYB; it
    never substitutes.
  • MCC assignment follows C-7. Conditional categories require the condition documented before
    activation.
  • The SOP-10 Appendix D checks are the onboarding-analyst subset of this document's standards.
  • Underwritten volume, ticket profile and chargeback expectation are written to the monitoring
    baseline and drive R-ACQ parameters.
  • Undisclosed prior terminations discovered later are SOP-22 events.
  • MCCs follow the card-scheme rules and the merchant’s actual activity, not the application alone.

SOP-16: Card programme limits and controls (stub)
Full version in wave 2. The points below bind now.



Purpose
Card limits by risk class, ATM caps, geographic controls, and the card-specific fraud interface.


Binding points
  • Limits and ATM caps per risk class live in the card limit register; values shall not be written into
    routines.
  • High-risk customers receive reduced ATM caps; changes route through the SOP-18 change-
    control pattern.
  • Card activation follows SOP-10/11 outcomes only; no card ships before the monitoring baseline
    exists.
  • KYC approval alone does not activate a card: fraud, security and authentication checks are
    completed first.

Purpose
Operational controls on the Crypto Service: counterparty wallet screening, travel-rule data handling
under Regulation (EU) 2023/1113, VASP counterparty due diligence, and the signal interface into R-
CRY rules.

  • Wallet screening runs on every Crypto Service transaction; sanctioned-wallet signals route to
    SOP-21 immediately.

SOP-21: Sanctions freeze and reporting (stub)
Full version in wave 2. The points below bind now.



Purpose
Execution of asset freezes on true matches and non-dismissable hits: immediate actions, reporting,
and ongoing administration.


Binding points
  • Freeze first, analyse second. No customer notification. No execution of pending transactions.
  • Freeze covers funds and services: accounts, cards, acquiring settlement and Crypto Service
    access simultaneously.
  • Reports to the competent authorities without delay; MLRO owns all handling thereafter.
  • Release only under GOV-03 section 9 licence handling.
  • Every freeze is logged with timestamps; SOP-19 and SOP-20 assessments run in parallel where
    suspicion exists.
  • A temporary operational block is kept separate from a legal asset freeze, and Finansinspektionen
    is the reporting authority for freezing actions.

SOP-22: Ongoing due diligence and review (stub)
Full version in wave 2. The points below bind now.



Purpose
Periodic reviews by risk class, event-driven reviews, and maintenance of customer information and
baselines.


Binding points
  • Intervals: high 12 months, medium 24, low 36; PEP files 12 regardless of class.
  • Event triggers include: ownership change, screening deltas, SOP-19 close-with-note closing
    decisions, returned contact channels, discovered prior terminations, and post-approval PEP or the
    high-risk category discoveries.
  • Reviews use T-4 and re-run SOP-14; outcomes route per the T-4 outcome set.
  • Overdue reviews appear by name in the MLRO report at 30 days past due.
  • Fixed review cycles never replace continuous and event-driven review, and an overdue review
    escalates from its due date.

Purpose
Risk-driven termination of relationships: grounds, decision, timing coordination with reporting,

Governance stubs: GOV-05 to GOV-09
Full versions in wave 2. The points below bind now.



GOV-05 Safeguarding
Customer funds are safeguarded at Norrbank AB under the segregation method; reconciliation daily;
safeguarding is out of AML scope but its counterparty risk appears in GOV-01 batch data. The
safeguarding scope is stated: which funds, which accounts, which reconciliation.


GOV-06 Internal audit charter
Independent examination per GOV-02 section 11; plan approved by the board; findings tracked to
closure. Internal audit is independent, works to a risk-based plan, and reports directly to the board.


GOV-07 Data protection interface
AML retention is a legal obligation prevailing over erasure for its period; conflicts route to the DPO
and MLRO jointly; access to SAR material is restricted per SOP-20 section 5. The erasure exception
is limited to data legally required for the applicable retention period, nothing wider.


GOV-08 Outsourcing and vendor governance
Screening, identity and infrastructure vendors are material arrangements: contracts, performance
review, exit plans; responsibility never transfers; vendor performance reviews feed the QC cycle.
DORA ICT arrangements are handled separately from other outsourcing, with classification, FI
notification where required, audit rights, subcontracting terms, and tested exit plans.


GOV-09 Product and change approval
New products, product areas, tokens and material feature changes require documented risk
assessment against GOV-01, MLRO sign-off, and board approval where appetite moves; C-8
approved list additions route here. Reviews cover Legal, Compliance, Risk, the DPO, the CTO, Finance
and safeguarding, and the FI is notified where a change affects the business model.

OP-01: Complaints handling (stub)
Full version internal per REG-D. The points below bind the FCP framework now.



Binding points
  • Complaints are received free of charge, registered, acknowledged and answered within the
    published time limits, with a register and root-cause reporting to management.
  • A complaint that reveals a possible framework breach is also an incident under GOV-02 section
    14.
  • A complaint touching examination, reporting or a freeze is answered without disclosing any of
    them; wording routes through the MLRO function per SOP-20 section 6.
  • If no answer can be given within 14 business days, the complainant is told when one is expected.

OP-02: Whistleblowing (stub)
Full version internal per REG-D. The points below bind now.



Binding points
  • An independent, confidential channel exists for reporting suspected breaches, open to all staff;
    reports may be made anonymously.
  • Reprisal against a reporter is prohibited and is itself an incident; investigations report to the
    board.
  • Suspicion of money laundering or terrorist financing may always be raised directly per SOP-19
    and T-5; the channels coexist and neither replaces the other.
  • The channel covers employees, contractors, trainees, management and active shareholders, with
    acknowledgement within 7 days and feedback within 3 months.

OP-04: Record retention schedule (stub)
Full version internal per REG-D. The points below bind now.



Binding points
  • FCP records retain five years from relationship end or transaction execution, ten where law
    requires or an authority orders, per GOV-02 section 10.
