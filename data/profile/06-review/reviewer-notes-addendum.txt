Reviewer notes, addendum after the shelf review
Raised by the Head of Legal & Compliance review of the build list; resolved as follows, corrections
welcome on each.


GOV-01A · Sanctions risk assessment
New annex closing the flagged gap. Wanted: exposure-path completeness for an EMI of this shape,
the inherent and residual ratings, and the three acceptance conditions in section 4.


PROG-01 · Programme of operations
New canon-level document; FI-reads-this-first logic adopted. Wanted: whether the eight sections
cover what a verksamhetsplan must, at reference depth, and anything materially missing for
coherence.


REG-D · Document register
The full internal shelf from the review is now referenced: corporate governance, ERM, compliance
policy and monitoring plan, fit and proper, conflicts, capital, accounting, ICT and DORA set,
communications, audit, remuneration, complaints, whistleblowing, training, retention, reporting, wind-
down, and the affiliate's CASP shelf as a separate entity boundary. Wanted: anything a Swedish EMI
shelf carries that the register still lacks, and whether instrument-level legal references (kept
deliberately without article numbers in the published register) are the right disclosure level.


Stubs v1.1
Four referenced-but-absent stubs added: complaints, whistleblowing, training, retention. Wanted:
binding-point accuracy.


Positions taken
English is canonical for publication; Swedish originals exist in-universe, and Swedish source material
becomes a difficulty feature in cases. The MiCA operating system is asserted by register reference,
not authored; the affiliate boundary is stated in PROG-01 section 6. Page-count is explicitly
subordinated to coherence, per the review's own principle.
