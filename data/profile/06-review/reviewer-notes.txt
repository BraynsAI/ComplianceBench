Reviewer notes: open questions per document
Collected from the working drafts. These do not appear in the pack documents. Rule on each;
corrections propagate to the affected document and, where marked, to the pilot rubrics.


GOV-01 · Business-wide risk assessment
Expert pass wanted on: ratings and their arguments (especially acquiring high, crypto very-high-
bounded-to-high, channel high-to-medium); the section 13 acceptance formulations against FI
practice; the conclusions C1 to C10 as binding style; the typology catalogue's realism; anything a
60-to-90-page BWRA carries that this structure still lacks, with the honest note that Mynta's
smaller-EMI calibration argues for the lower half of that range.


GOV-02 · AML/CTF policy
Expert pass wanted on: the 5.1 to 5.3 duty enumerations against FFFS 2017:11; the occasional-
transaction position; the 7.5 and 7.6 statements; the section 13 control-plan and self-assessment
design; and any statement a Swedish EMI board policy carries that this draft still lacks.


GOV-03 · Sanctions policy
Expert pass wanted on: the OFAC stance formulation; the 50%-rule treatment and its freeze-first
ordering; list-update SLA realism; and whether Swedish practice expects an explicit sectoral-
measures section (dual-use, trade restrictions) at an EMI of this profile or leaves it at the transaction-
screening level.


GOV-04 · Customer risk appetite
Expert pass wanted on: the prohibited list against real Swedish EMI practice; the metric values in
section 5 as plausible board numbers; the restricted-category conditions; and whether adult content
beyond live-streaming, CBD, tobacco and similar conditional sectors should appear here rather than
resolving through C-7.


SOP-10 · KYB onboarding routine
Expert pass wanted on: the section 5 matrix against real Swedish practice (especially stiftelse and
ekonomisk förening rows); the 7.2 language and legalisation standards; SDD scope in section 9; the
review intervals in Appendix C; SLA values in S1.2 and S1.4; and anything a real 20-page KYB routine
contains that this one still lacks. S4.2, S4.3, S4.5 and S5.1 are pinned by the pilot case; changes
there propagate to the Halvix rubric.


SOP-11 · KYC onboarding routine
Expert pass wanted on: the automated approval and decline boundaries in S6.2 and S6.3; the
address non-verification position at S2.5; the sole-trader interface with SOP-10; the 2% sampling
rate for straight-through approvals; and the Appendix B list against what consumer desks actually
see.
