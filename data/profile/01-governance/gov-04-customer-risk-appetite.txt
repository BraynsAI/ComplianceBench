1 · Purpose
This statement says which customers and activities Mynta accepts and which it does not, decided in
advance, so that the answer never depends on commercial pressure in an individual customer case.
Onboarding, product decisions and periodic review shall all follow it. Prohibited means the customer
is declined, at whatever stage the ground is discovered, and no one at any level can grant an
exception. Restricted means the customer can be accepted only when the stated conditions are met.
The lists derive from GOV-01's assessment and the board's tolerance; changing them is a board
decision through this document, never a file-level judgement.


2 · Prohibited
Mynta does not onboard, and exits on discovery:

  • Shell banks, and institutions permitting their accounts to be used by shell banks.
  • Unlicensed money-service businesses and unlicensed crypto-asset service providers, in any
    jurisdiction where their activity requires a licence.
  • Companies that have issued bearer shares. Exception: the shares are deposited with a regulated
    custodian so ownership can be verified, and this is documented to SOP-10 standards.
  • Companies using nominee shareholders or nominee directors, where the real principals are not
    fully disclosed and documented to SOP-10 standards. Where this means due diligence cannot be
    completed, the law itself already prohibits the relationship; this line restates it as appetite.

4 · Product and concentration appetite
  • No cash. Mynta offers no cash services. The only cash touchpoint is ATM withdrawals with issued
    cards, inside SOP-16 limits.
  • Crypto limits. Crypto Service volume is capped at 5% of institutional processed volume and per-
    customer per risk class per C-8's register values; tokens are approved listed per C-8 with

   additions only through GOV-09. Breaching a bound suspends Crypto Service growth per GOV-01
   conclusion C3.
  • Acquiring concentration. No single merchant above 4% of institutional processed volume without
    board notification; no MCC category above 20% of acquiring volume without MLRO review of the
    concentration.
  • Cross-border flow concentration. Sustained cross-border flow patterns inconsistent with the
    customer base's profile trigger review under SOP-18 and, where structural, a GOV-01 event
    review.
  • Combinations. Where several risk factors combine, they are assessed jointly, not by simply taking
    the strictest single rule; the joint assessment can land stricter than any single factor. One
    combination is outside appetite entirely: a complex structure, plus the high-risk category
    connection, plus crypto-dominant expected activity.

6 · Application and attestation
Enforcement points: SOP-10 S1.3 at intake, SOP-15 at underwriting, SOP-22 at review, SOP-23 at
exit. The declined, abandoned and exited registers make prohibitions durable across re-applications.
Product owners attest annually that their products operate within this statement, as part of the
section 13 control plan in GOV-02. Exceptions to section 2 do not exist at any level; a case for
holding something prohibited is a case for the board to change this document, prospectively and on
record. Restricted-category conditions are not negotiable per file; failing the condition means
declining.
