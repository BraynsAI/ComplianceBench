1 · Purpose
This policy states how Mynta AB prevents its products from being used for money laundering and
terrorist financing, and assigns accountability for each obligation. It is the parent document of the
financial crime prevention framework. the elevated category routines (SOP-10 to SOP-25) implement
it. The registers (REG-C) hold its operating values. Where a routine and this policy diverge, this policy
prevails and the divergence shall be corrected through SOP-25.

2 · Scope of obliged activities
Mynta AB is an obliged entity under penningtvattslagen as an e-money institution. The framework
covers all of Mynta's activities: issuance and redemption of e-money, account and payment services,
card issuing, merchant acquiring, and the distribution of crypto-asset services provided by Mynta
Digital AB. The distribution activity is within scope in full: Mynta performs its own due diligence,
monitoring and reporting for the customers it serves, irrespective of the affiliate's parallel obligations
as a CASP.

Mynta offers no cash services, no correspondent relationships, no trade finance, and no occasional
transactions to non-customers. Should any such activity be proposed, GOV-09 requires assessment
against this framework before launch.


3 · Definitions

Money laundering           As defined in penningtvattslagen 1 kap 6 §, including self-laundering and the
                           handling of property derived from crime.

Terrorist financing        As defined in penningtvattslagen 1 kap 7 §, irrespective of the origin of the funds.

Business relationship      A relationship with an element of duration, established when an application is
                           approved under SOP-10 or SOP-11.

Beneficial owner           Per SOP-10 section 3. The identified-not-declared rule applies throughout the
                           framework.

Framework                  This policy, GOV-01 and GOV-03 to GOV-09, the SOP series, REG-C and the
                           templates.

Incident                   A breach of this framework, a control failure, or a near miss, per section 14.



4 · Policy statements
  • Mynta shall not be used for money laundering or terrorist financing. The measures in this
    framework exist to prevent, detect and report abuse.
  • The approach is risk-based on the foundation of GOV-01. Higher assessed risk shall always result
    in more extensive measures.
  • No commercial objective overrides an obligation in this framework. Conflicts shall be escalated to
    the MLRO.
  • Known control limitations are documented in GOV-01 and shall be taken into account in decisions
    that rely on the affected controls.
  • Every decision under this framework shall be reconstructable from records alone.


5 · Governance and accountability

5.1 The board
The board shall: approve GOV-01 and its revisions, the risk appetite in GOV-04, and material changes
to the overall risk profile; appoint the sarskilt utsedd befattningshavare; receive the MLRO report
quarterly and the annual control-plan results; and, together with the CEO, ensure the function is

resourced for its task. This policy is adopted by the CEO and presented to the board; annual approval
of the policy itself is an internal Mynta standard, not an FFFS requirement, and is recorded as such.
Board minutes shall evidence each decision.

5.2 Sarskilt utsedd befattningshavare
A member of executive management is designated under FFFS 2017:11 as responsible for
penningtvattslagen compliance. The designated executive shall: ensure the measures the law
requires are carried out, and that the framework is implemented and maintained; and ensure
deficiencies and material incidents reach the board without filtering. Resourcing is decided by the
CEO and the board. The annual control-plan report is issued by the central function; the designated
executive receives it and answers to the board for the actions it triggers.

5.3 Centralt funktionsansvarig
The central function holder (MLRO) is appointed by the board on the designated executive’s
proposal. The function monitors compliance with this framework, assesses whether the controls
work, advises the business, makes recommendations, and issues an annual independent report to
the board. It does not own the framework or the controls it reviews: ownership sits with the first line
and the designated executive, so the review stays independent. The mandate includes:
independence from business lines and from revenue targets; unrestricted access to all information,
systems and personnel; direct access to the board; authority to decide reporting to Finanspolisen
without any business approval; ownership of GOV-01, this framework's documents, the registers and
the deviation and incident registers; and the quarterly MLRO report. A deputy is appointed with full
authority in the MLRO's absence. The MLRO's removal or a material reduction of the mandate
requires a board decision with the reasons minuted.

5.4 Three lines
First line: business and operational teams own their risks and execute the routines. Second line: the
compliance function under the MLRO sets the framework, advises, monitors and challenges. Third
line: independent examination per section 13. A person shall not review their own decisions in any
line.

5.5 All staff
Every employee shall know the parts of the framework their role touches, complete the training in
section 11, raise suspicion per SOP-19 or T-5 rather than assessing it away, and observe the
prohibition in section 9.4. These duties are terms of employment.


6 · Risk assessment and classification
GOV-01 is the institution's risk assessment under penningtvattslagen 2 kap. It shall be revised
annually and on its event triggers, and board-approved. Customers shall be classified per SOP-14
into low, medium and high risk. Classification consequences are set in C-6 (authority), SOP-22
(review intervals), SOP-18 (monitoring intensity) and the routines' verification standards.
Classifications shall reconcile to GOV-01 per its conclusion C8. New products, services, technologies
and channels shall be risk-assessed under GOV-09 before launch, and GOV-01 shall be updated
where the assessment changes the institution's risk picture.

7 · Customer due diligence

7.1 When due diligence is required
Before a business relationship is established; when doubts arise about the veracity or adequacy of
previously obtained information; and whenever money laundering or terrorist financing is suspected,
irrespective of any exemption or threshold. Mynta’s products are built so that transactions are
executed for customers only, and requests for occasional transactions from non-customers shall be
declined. This position does not set aside the law: should an occasional transaction nonetheless
occur or be attempted, the statutory due diligence, monitoring and reporting requirements apply to it
in full.

7.2 Content
Due diligence comprises: identification and verification of the customer; identification of beneficial
owners and risk-proportionate verification, including understanding of ownership and control
structure; assessment and, as warranted, documentation of the purpose and nature of the
relationship; and ongoing monitoring per section 9. The measures are set operationally in SOP-10,
SOP-11 and their rules.

7.3 Enhanced measures
Enhanced due diligence per SOP-13 shall be applied: where penningtvattslagen requires it, including
PEP connections and establishment or transactions involving high-risk third countries identified by
the Commission; and in the situations C-9 designates. The C-9 triggers are Mynta requirements in
addition to law.

7.4 Simplified measures
Simplified due diligence may be applied only where the law permits and GOV-01 supports low risk,
per SOP-10 section 9 and SOP-11. Simplification shall never omit screening, purpose assessment or
risk classification.

7.5 Politically exposed persons
Mynta shall determine whether customers, beneficial owners and their associates are PEPs, family
members or known close associates. Confirmed connections require enhanced measures, source of
wealth and funds assessment, approval by the authorised senior decision-maker the governance
framework designates, and enhanced monitoring, per SOP-10 section 8 and SOP-13. A person shall
be treated as a PEP for at least 18 months after leaving the function, and longer where the
relationship remains high risk.

7.6 High-risk third countries
Relationships and transactions involving high-risk third countries per the EU list shall receive
enhanced measures. The statutory trigger is that the customer is established in a third country the
European Commission has identified as high risk; those relationships receive the enhanced
measures the law requires. C-2 the high-risk category is broader than the EU list; the additional the
high-risk category countries are a Mynta risk-appetite requirement and are handled as such, distinct
from the statutory high-risk-third-country treatment.

7.7 The prohibition
Where due diligence cannot be completed, the relationship shall not be established. Where it exists,
it shall not continue, and transactions shall not be executed, subject to the statutory carve-outs.
Termination on these grounds follows SOP-23, and the attempted or terminated relationship shall be
assessed under SOP-20. No level of the organisation may waive this prohibition.

7.8 Reliance
Mynta does not apply the penningtvattslagen reliance provisions. Vendors are outsourced tools
under GOV-08, and responsibility remains Mynta's. Information from introducers may inform
assessment; it shall not substitute for any measure.


8 · Sanctions
Sanctions obligations are governed by GOV-03 on their own legal base. Screening under SOP-12
serves both regimes. On any confirmed or non-dismissable sanctions match, SOP-21 applies
immediately and this policy's monitoring and reporting provisions run in parallel where suspicion
exists.


9 · Monitoring, examination and reporting

9.1 Monitoring
Customer activity shall be monitored against the recorded baseline, the risk class and known
typologies, across all product areas in total, per SOP-18. Monitoring baselines are mandatory at
activation; activation without a baseline is prohibited.

9.2 Examination
Deviations and indicators shall be examined per SOP-19 with the depth the circumstances require.
Examination shall be recorded to the reconstruction standard. Closing an alert without genuine
examination is a breach of this policy.

9.3 Reporting
Where reasonable grounds to suspect remain after examination, the MLRO function shall report to
Finanspolisen without undue delay per SOP-20. The decision is the MLRO's alone. There is no de
minimis. Attempted relationships and refused transactions are assessed on the same standard. A
suspicious transaction not yet executed shall be refrained from until reported, unless refraining is
impossible or would reveal the examination, in which case it may be executed and reported
immediately after, with reasons recorded.

9.4 Prohibited disclosure
No employee shall inform the customer or any third party that examination, reporting or a freeze
consideration has occurred or may occur, or act in a way from which this can be inferred. The
statutory exceptions are exercised only by the MLRO. Breach is a criminal exposure for the individual
and gross misconduct at Mynta.

9.5 Authority cooperation
Inquiries and orders from Finanspolisen, Finansinspektionen and other competent authorities shall
be answered completely and within the demanded time, through the MLRO function. Business
teams shall route any authority contact to the MLRO function unanswered.


10 · Records
Due diligence records, transaction records, examinations, reports and decision rationales shall be
retained five years from relationship end or transaction execution, ten where law requires or an
authority orders. Records shall be retrievable per the routines' standards and structured so
compliance with each obligation is demonstrable. Retention under this framework is a legal
obligation and prevails over erasure requests for its duration; the interface is governed by GOV-07.
SAR material is segregated with restricted access per SOP-20 section 5.


11 · Training and suitability
All staff shall complete AML/CTF training on joining and annually. Depth is role-based: onboarding,
monitoring and screening roles train on their routines and current typologies; management trains on
governance and accountability; the board receives an annual briefing. Completion shall be registered
and attested. Training content shall be maintained against GOV-01's typology picture and QC
findings. Persons in framework-relevant roles shall be suitability-checked at hiring per the HR
procedure, including relevant background verification permitted by law.


12 · Group application and outsourcing
Mynta applies its standards in the group context where they are the stronger standard. Coordination
with Mynta Digital AB covers shared customers, the Crypto Service interface, travel-rule data flows
and mutual escalation, and shall be documented between the entities. Each entity's legal obligations
remain its own. Outsourced services are governed by GOV-08: material arrangements require
contracts covering audit and data access, performance review, and exit planning. Responsibility for
compliance is never outsourced.


13 · Internal control and independent review
Three lines, plainly: the first line does the operational work and its own quality control; the second
line, the central function, monitors compliance and reports annually; the third line is independent
review. Within that, the second line shall operate an annual control plan over the framework: the QC
programmes in the routines, register reviews, effectiveness monitoring, and thematic checks
selected from GOV-01's risk picture. Results are reported quarterly in the MLRO report and annually
as a consolidated self-assessment signed by the designated executive. Independent examination
proportionate to Mynta's size shall review the framework's design and operation on a board-
approved cycle, per GOV-06, with findings tracked to closure in board reporting.


14 · Incidents, breaches and discipline
Framework breaches, control failures and near misses are incidents. Incidents shall be recorded,
root-caused, corrected and reported upward proportionate to severity. The MLRO assesses whether

an incident requires notifying Finansinspektionen; the decision is taken together with the designated
executive, and the board is informed. An undocumented deviation from a routine is an incident
regardless of outcome. Breaches attributable to individuals are handled through the disciplinary
process. Prohibited disclosure and wilful non-reporting constitute gross misconduct. Reports
through the whistleblowing channel are protected, and reprisal is itself an incident.
