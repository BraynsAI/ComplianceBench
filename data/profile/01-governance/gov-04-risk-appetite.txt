1 · Purpose
This statement says what Mynta will and will not hold, in advance, so the decision is never made
under commercial pressure at a single file. It binds onboarding, product decisions and review.
Prohibited means declined, at whatever stage it is discovered, with no exception path at any level.
Restricted means held only under the named conditions. The lists derive from GOV-01's assessment
and the board's tolerance; changing them is a board decision through this document, never a file-
level judgement.


2 · Prohibited
Mynta does not onboard, and exits on discovery:

  • Shell banks, and institutions permitting their accounts to be used by shell banks.
  • Unlicensed money-service businesses and unlicensed crypto-asset service providers, in any
    jurisdiction where their activity requires a licence.
  • Entities with bearer shares, unless immobilised with a regulated custodian and evidenced to
    SOP-10 standards.
  • Nominee shareholding or directorship arrangements without full disclosure of the principals to
    SOP-10 evidence standards.
  • Entities whose ownership or control cannot be established to this framework's standards, per the
    GOV-02 section 7.7 prohibition.
  • Any person or entity with prohibited-jurisdiction nexus per C-2, at any chain layer.
  • Trusts and comparable foreign legal arrangements as acquiring customers; as account customers
    only with MLRO pre-approval per SOP-10 section 5.
  • Business lines: unlicensed gambling; weapons, ammunition and dual-use trade; adult live-
    streaming services; sanctioned-sector activity per GOV-03.
  • Anonymous products of any kind; no product is offered without completed due diligence.

4 · Product and concentration appetite
  • No cash. Mynta operates no cash products or cash access beyond scheme-standard ATM
    issuance under SOP-16 limits.
  • Crypto bounds. Ramp volume is capped at 5% of institutional processed volume and per-
    customer per risk class per C-8's register values; tokens are allowlisted per C-8 with additions
    only through GOV-09. Breaching a bound suspends ramp growth per GOV-01 conclusion C3.
  • Acquiring concentration. No single merchant above 4% of institutional processed volume without
    board notification; no MCC category above 20% of acquiring volume without MLRO review of the
    concentration.
  • Corridor concentration. Sustained corridor patterns inconsistent with the customer base's profile
    trigger review under SOP-18 and, where structural, a GOV-01 event review.
  • Combinations. Where several rules apply, the strictest one wins. One combination is outside
    appetite entirely: a complex structure, plus a Tier 3 nexus, plus crypto-dominant expected activity.

6 · Application and attestation
Enforcement points: SOP-10 S1.3 at intake, SOP-15 at underwriting, SOP-22 at review, SOP-23 at
exit. The declined, abandoned and exited registers make prohibitions durable across re-applications.
Product owners attest annually that their products operate within this statement, as part of the
section 13 control plan in GOV-02. Exceptions to section 2 do not exist at any level; a case for
holding something prohibited is a case for the board to change this document, prospectively and on
record. Restricted-category conditions are not negotiable per file; failing the condition means
declining.


7 · Review
Reviewed with GOV-01 annually and on its event triggers. Metric performance is reported quarterly.
Changes state their GOV-01 basis. Appetite shall not be widened ahead of the assessment that
justifies it.
