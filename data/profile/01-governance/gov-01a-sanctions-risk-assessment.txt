1 · Purpose
This assessment identifies where Mynta can encounter sanctioned persons, entities and activity,
rates the exposure before and after controls, and states the conditions under which the residual
position is accepted. It exists as a distinct assessment because the obligation is strict in character:
intent is irrelevant, thresholds are absent, and a single missed true match is a breach.

3 · Controls relied on
Screening on identified persons at onboarding, delta and transaction level (SOP-12, C-4); list-update
service levels per GOV-03 section 10; SOP-10 ownership-structure evidence standards as the
discovery mechanism for the 50% rule, per GOV-01 conclusion C4; wallet screening and travel-rule
data (SOP-17); cross-border flow and reference rules (SOP-18); immediate freeze-and-report on true
matches (SOP-21); C-2 prohibited-band enforcement at any layer; annual screening effectiveness
testing with seeding.


4 · Residual position and acceptance
The residual position is medium, carried by the ownership-and-control, crypto and circumvention
paths. It is accepted on three standing conditions. First: ownership-structure evidence quality does
not degrade. A SOP-10 QC deterioration is a sanctions control failure, not only an onboarding one.
Second: the list-update and screening-coverage service levels hold, documented in the update log
and in annual testing. Third: the Crypto Service operates inside its C-8 limits. Breach of a condition
triggers out-of-cycle review of this assessment and of GOV-01 section 11.


5 · Review and triggers
Reviewed annually with GOV-01. Out-of-cycle triggers: material regime change or new programme; a
missed true match or a near-miss found in testing; SOP-10 QC findings at critical grade touching
ownership-structure evidence; C-8 bound breach; and supervisory communication on sanctions
controls. Changes are recorded in the GOV-01 Appendix D change history with this annex's own
entries.
