1 · Purpose and principles
This routine turns examined suspicion into a report Finanspolisen can use, and governs everything
around that act: the decision, the drafting standard, the filing, the aftermath, and the register. The
following principles govern. The decision is the MLRO's alone: no business approval is sought and
none may be demanded. The threshold is reasonable grounds to suspect. That means a basis the
MLRO can put into words after examination. It is well below proof, and it does not depend on the
amount: no minimum value exists. Attempts are reportable: a declined or abandoned relationship, or
a refused transaction, is assessed the same way. Meddelandeförbudet governs every action this
routine touches.


2 · The decision
S1        Referral arrives with the SOP-19 case record or equivalent context. The MLRO reads the
          case to the examination standard. Further work is requested only where a specific gap
          blocks the decision, because the statutory clock, without undue delay, is already running.

S2        Decide: report, or do not report. Both outcomes are recorded with reasons in the register; a
          non-report rationale states why the grounds examined do not reach the threshold. IF new

S3        Pending transactions. IF a suspicious transaction has not been executed, THEN it is not

          recorded. This ordering is law, not preference.


3 · Drafting standard
The report also states the current balances, whether funds are still held, whether the transaction
was stopped, the intended recipient, and where the money went.

The report is written for a reader with no prior context, and its quality is measured by whether that
reader can act on it. Required content:

  • Subjects: every relevant person and entity with identifiers, roles and relationships, including those
    Mynta identified but the customer never declared.
  • The relationship: products, opening date, stated purpose, the S3 baseline, risk class and any EDD
    history.
  • The activity: chronological narrative with amounts, dates, counterparties, accounts and product
    areas; totals and patterns stated, not left for the reader to compute.
  • The suspicion: what makes this activity suspicious against this customer's profile and which
    typologies it resembles, with the indicators listed; facts and assessment kept visibly separate.
  • Attachments: the documents that let the reader verify the narrative: statements, due-diligence
    extracts, the examination record where useful.

Style rules: plain language, no internal jargon or system names without explanation, no speculation
presented as fact, and no copy-paste of alert text in place of narrative. The deputy MLRO reviews
every draft before filing except where absence makes the MLRO and drafter the same person, which
is recorded.


4 · Filing
S4        File through goAML under Mynta's registration, using the report type the situation calls for;
          capture the acknowledgment and reference into the register. Technical filing failure is
          escalated the same day and the report is delivered by the fallback channel Finanspolisen
          designates, with the failure documented.

S5        After filing. The relationship decision is taken deliberately: continue with enhanced
          monitoring per SOP-18/22, restrict products, or exit via SOP-23. Exit timing is coordinated so
          that the action itself does not disclose the report. Nothing in customer-facing handling
          changes in tone or content because a report exists.

S6        FIU follow-up. Questions and disclosure orders from Finanspolisen are answered by the
          MLRO function within the demanded time, completely and only through this routine;
          business teams receiving any authority contact route it here untouched.

5 · The register and access
Access to reports is named and least-privilege, logged, and checked on a set cycle. Staff outside the
function see only the actions they must take, never the report itself.

The internal reporting register records referrals, decisions with reasons, drafts, filings with
references, FIU correspondence and relationship decisions. Access is restricted to the MLRO
function; existence of a report on a customer is visible to other systems only as the enhanced-
handling flags it produces, never as the fact itself. Register extracts appear in the MLRO report as
counts and themes, not names.


6 · Tipping
Prohibited disclosure includes telling the customer or any third party that examination has occurred,
that a report has been or may be made, or anything from which that could be inferred, including
through decline wording, exit letters, or unusual handling. Lawful exceptions (disclosure to the
supervisor, and the narrow statutory group and inter-institution cases) are exercised only by the
MLRO after checking the conditions. Breach is a criminal exposure for the individual and gross
misconduct at Mynta.


6b · After the report
A report does not automatically end the relationship. Continue, restrict or exit is decided separately,
considering any police instructions, frozen funds and the tipping-off risk.


7 · Records, timeliness and quality
SAR files follow GOV-02 §10 retention in the restricted store. Timeliness is tracked referral-to-
decision and decision-to-filing, with medians and outliers in the MLRO report. Annually, the MLRO
and deputy run a look-back on a sample of filings and non-report decisions for consistency of
threshold application and drafting quality, The conclusions feed training. Where the threshold has
drifted, it is recalibrated, on record.
