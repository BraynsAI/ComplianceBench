1 · Purpose and scope
Sole traders (enskild näringsidkare): the business relationship is onboarded under SOP-10 section 5,
with the proprietor’s identity handled to this document’s standards.

This routine sets out how Mynta identifies, verifies and assesses natural persons before an account is
opened. It covers consumer applications and sole traders. When natural persons act for a legal-
person customer (signatories, owners), they are verified to the standards in this routine; SOP-10
section 7 points here. Steps are mandatory unless marked conditional. No product shall be activated
before the section 5 procedure has concluded with an approval at the correct authority level.

Mynta offers account-based services only. It does not execute occasional transactions for people
who are not customers, so the threshold-based due diligence regime for occasional transactions is
not used. Any request for a one-off service without an account shall be declined.


2 · Legal basis
Penningtvättslagen (2017:630) 3 kap; FFFS 2017:11; the EBA risk factor guidelines. Rule values
referenced in this routine (C-2, C-4, C-9) are Mynta requirements and apply in addition to law.

3 · Roles and authority
The onboarding flow is automated for data capture, verification and screening. Files that pass all
automated checks and classify as low risk are approved without manual intervention (S6.2). All other
files route to the onboarding team. Authority follows C-6: low risk, analyst or automated approval;
medium, senior analyst; high and all PEP files, MLRO. Decline authority in the automated flow is
limited to the objective grounds in S6.3; all other declines require an analyst.


4 · Application data
S1.1       Capture: full name, personnummer or date of birth and place of birth, citizenship(s), country
           of residence, residential address, contact details, and the applicant's employment or main
           occupation.

S1.2       Capture expected use: purpose of the account, expected monthly incoming and outgoing
           volumes, main counterparty types and countries, source of funds category (salary, business
           income, savings, benefits, other with free text), and whether crypto Crypto Service access is
           requested with expected share of activity.

S1.3       Capture the PEP self-declaration: whether the applicant is a PEP, a family member of a PEP
           or a known close associate, as defined in SOP-10 section 8. The declaration shall not replace
           screening.

S1.4       Applicants must be 18 or older. Applications from persons under 18 shall be declined. Mynta
           does not offer guardianship or minor accounts.


5 · Identification and verification
A residence that has not been verified cannot on its own drive an automated decline.
S2.1       Swedish residents with personnummer: verify identity with BankID. The BankID assertion
           satisfies identity verification without further documents.

S2.2       Other applicants: verify with a passport or EEA national identity card through the identity
           vendor's document and liveness flow. The document must be valid, machine-readable and
           captured live. The liveness check must pass at the vendor's standard assurance level.

S2.3       IF verification fails twice, THEN route to manual review. In manual review, two outcomes are
           possible. The reviewer may accept a certified copy of the identity document per SOP-10
           section 7.3, together with a repeated liveness check. Where identity cannot be established,
           the reviewer shall decline. Look at why verification failed. If the cause is document quality,
           help the applicant capture a better image. If the cause is a mismatch between the document
           and the person, treat that as an indicator and record it.

S2.4       Consistency checks: the name on the verified document must match the application name.
           Transliteration differences are acceptable where the identity vendor confirms the match. A
           different name is a failed verification, and S2.3 applies.

S2.5       Address is recorded from the application and is not separately verified at onboarding. IF the
           risk classification is high, THEN address verification per SOP-13 scoping applies.

6 · Screening, classification and decision
S3.1       Screen the applicant per SOP-12 against sanctions, PEP data and designated adverse media
           before any approval. The file is not complete until the screening results are on it.

S3.2       IF the PEP self-declaration and screening disagree, THEN the file routes to manual review. A
           confirmed PEP connection routes per SOP-10 section 8: high risk, SOP-13 measures, MLRO
           decision. If the applicant denied the connection and screening confirms it, record that as an
           indicator.

S4.1       Classify per SOP-14. C-9 triggers route to SOP-13 before decision. For consumers the
           common triggers are the high-risk category residence and requested crypto share at or
           above 30%.

S5.1       Reasonable: compare the expected volumes with the stated occupation and source of funds
           category. IF expected activity is materially above what the stated profile supports, THEN the
           file routes to manual review and the analyst shall request clarification or classify upward
           with a recorded reason.

S6.1       Manual decisions record rationale to the SOP-10 S7.2 standard.

S6.2       Automated approval applies only where all of the following hold: verification passed at S2.1
           or S2.2 on the first pass, screening returned no hits requiring the decision on hits, no C-9
           trigger is present, the SOP-14 class is low, and the reasonableness check at S5.1 passed. All
           other files require an analyst.

S6.3       Automated decline applies only on objective grounds: age under 18, failed identity
           verification after S2.3, or prohibited-jurisdiction residence per C-2. Decline communication
           follows S7.3.


7 · Activation and handover
S7.1       On approval, activate only the products applied for. Crypto Crypto Service access is a
           separate flag and requires the S1.2 crypto data to be complete.

S7.2       Write the monitoring baseline before activation: expected volumes, counterparty countries,
           source of funds category, crypto share, risk class and review date. Activation without a
           complete baseline is prohibited.

S7.3       Declines shall be communicated without disclosing screening results, suspicion or internal
           assessments. The analyst shall assess whether a declined attempt is reportable per SOP-20.


8 · Ongoing data
At onboarding, the customer is told about the duty to keep their information current. Identity re-
verification is required on legal name change and on document expiry where the original verification
relied on that document. Periodic refresh of customer data follows SOP-22 by risk class. A returned
mail flag, a bounced contact channel or a failed re-verification shall be treated as an SOP-22 event.

9 · Records
The onboarding record contains: application data, verification evidence and vendor references,
screening records per SOP-12 section 6, classification inputs per SOP-14, decision and rationale, and
the monitoring baseline. Retention follows GOV-02 section 10. Declined and abandoned applications
are retained on the same basis, and abandoned applications follow the SOP-10 section 14.4 handling.


10 · Quality control
Monthly re-review of a random selection: 2% of automated approvals and 10% of manual approvals
are re-reviewed against this routine, graded per the SOP-10 section 13 scale. All PEP files and all
overrides receive 100% second review before decision. Findings feed training and, where systematic,
a routine revision through SOP-25. Vendor verification performance (pass rates, fraud catches, false
rejects) is reviewed quarterly with the vendor under GOV-08.

Appendix B · Indicators at consumer onboarding
  • Applicant data reused across applications: shared device, address or contact details with
    unrelated applicants.
  • Document and liveness mismatch, or repeated near-miss verification attempts.
  • Expected volumes inconsistent with stated occupation.
  • Immediate crypto-dominant intent combined with a newly issued identity document.
  • Requests to open multiple accounts in short sequence.
  • Third party visibly directing the application during liveness capture.

Indicators shall be recorded on the file. Two or more indicators route the file to manual review
regardless of automated check results.
