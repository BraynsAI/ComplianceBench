The institution's governing-document shelf. Status PUBLISHED means the document is included in
the open benchmark pack; INTERNAL means it exists in the institution's universe, is referenced by
published documents, and is not part of the open pack. Internal documents are maintained in the
institution's document system with the same version discipline. Legal bases are recorded at
instrument level in this register; provision-level mapping lives in the internal legal register.

Language: English is governing for the published pack. Swedish originals of governing documents
are maintained in-universe per Swedish supervisory practice; where a case supplies Swedish source
material, the English framework governs interpretation.

Mynta Digital AB, CASP framework (separate entity, internal)
The affiliate maintains its own governing-document shelf under MiCA and Regulation (EU)
2025/305, including its programme of operations, custody and administration framework, transfer-
services policy, market-abuse prevention, pricing and communications policies, sustainability
disclosure procedure, and its own wind-down plan. That shelf is governed, versioned and supervised
on the affiliate's side of the group boundary described in PROG-01 section 6, and is not part of this
pack. The published interface documents are GOV-02 section 12, SOP-17 and REG-C C-8.


Register governance
The MLRO maintains this register. A published document is added, amended or retired only with a
register entry; an internal document's existence claim is itself version-controlled here. On any
conflict between a published document and an internal one on financial-crime matters, the published
FCP framework governs.
