C-2 · Jurisdiction categories
Composition rule: prohibited = (a) jurisdictions and territories that must be treated as prohibited
under the sanctions regimes binding on Mynta, EU, UN as implemented in EU law, and Swedish
national measures, per GOV-03 section 2, plus (b) board-decided exclusions beyond the legal
minimum. Russia and Belarus sit in (b) as board decisions; Cuba reflects designated OFAC exposure
only, not an EU or UN obligation, and is prohibited only on a separate board decision, per the GOV-03
three-way split. The high-risk category = EU high-risk third countries plus FATF lists plus board and
MLRO additions. The elevated category = jurisdictions requiring extra checks based on actual risk.
The standard category = all others. Snapshot as at 10 September 2026; the live list is maintained in
the register with a source reference per entry.

C-3 · Ownership structure documentation depth

Value                             Standard: ownership structure documented to identify all persons above 25%. High
                                  risk: ownership structure documented from 10%, all paths.

Change authority                  MLRO. Review at least annually.



C-4 · Screening the decision on hits
On a confirmed sanctions match, the relevant funds and assets are frozen immediately per SOP-21
and the matter is reported to the competent authority without delay. The customer is not notified.
This applies whoever detects the match and at whatever stage.

Values                            Similarity threshold 85%. First-line dismiss requires two or more mismatched
                                  secondary identifiers with recorded rationale. Secondary identifier order of weight:
                                  date of birth, nationality or registration country, personal or organisational number,
                                  address. Sanctions hits that cannot be dismissed are true matches for handling
                                  purposes.

Change authority                  MLRO, with vendor test evidence per SOP-12 section 7.
